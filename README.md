In this section, I list macroeconomic research projects that utilize advanced data analytics to solve complex financial cases.

### USD Strength & Inflation Transmission — Emerging Africa
**Goal:​** To analyze the degree and speed at which USD strength transmits to domestic inflation across major African economies.

**Code:​** [`project_1_fx_inflation`](https://github.com/Acube9ja/Africa-Macro-Finance-Portfolio/tree/master/project_1_fx_inflation)

**Description:​** This project examines the correlation between the US Dollar index and inflation rates in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, and Egypt (2006–2023). It involves cross-correlation lag analysis to determine how quickly exchange rate shocks impact consumer prices.

**Skills:​** Time-series analysis, cross-correlation, financial modeling, data cleaning.

**Technology:​** Python (Pandas, Statsmodels, Matplotlib), wbgapi, FRED API.

**Results:​** Analysis revealed that Nigeria shows the strongest immediate pass-through (r=0.68), while Kenya and South Africa exhibit structural buffers that delay the impact, reflecting differences in import dependency.

### Debt Sustainability Analysis — Sub-Saharan Africa
**Goal:​** To identify fiscal risk zones by comparing GDP growth rates against debt accumulation and revenue bases.

**Code:​** [`project_2_debt_sustainability`](https://github.com/Acube9ja/Africa-Macro-Finance-Portfolio/tree/master/project_2_debt_sustainability%20%E2%86%92%20notebooks)

**Description:​** A comparative study of debt-to-GDP ratios and government revenue across Sub-Saharan Africa (2010–2023). The project maps fiscal risk to identify countries where debt servicing is becoming unsustainable relative to their revenue-generating capacity.

**Skills:​** Risk assessment, data visualization, comparative analysis, fiscal policy research.

**Technology:​** Python, Pandas, wbgapi, Matplotlib.

**Results:​** Angola was identified as a high-risk outlier with a debt gap of 10.5pp, while the data for Zambia showed stress patterns that accurately preceded its 2020 default.

### Nigeria Macro Decomposition
**Goal:​** To test the relationship between non-oil GDP growth and employment gains in Nigeria.

**Code:​** [`project_3_nigeria_macro`](https://github.com/Acube9ja/Africa-Macro-Finance-Portfolio/tree/master/project_3_nigeria_macro)

**Description:​** This project decomposes Nigeria’s GDP into oil and non-oil components. It uses regression analysis to see if the growth in the non-oil sector (services, agriculture) has a statistically significant impact on reducing unemployment figures.

**Skills:​** GDP decomposition, hypothesis testing, exploratory data analysis (EDA).

**Technology:​** Python, wbgapi, FRED API, Matplotlib.

**Results:​** The findings indicate a "jobless growth" phenomenon; despite the GDP nearly doubling, unemployment rose steadily, suggesting structural inefficiencies in the non-oil sector's labor absorption.
